## About Our Research

Many organisms share a remarkable survival strategy: the ability to adjust their activity, metabolism, and behavior in response to the seasons, as well as mechanisms such as rapid underwater attachment and detachment. Our group focuses on the seasonal and temperature adaptation of animals such as medaka, and on the underwater adhesion abilities of pygmy squid, aiming to uncover the molecular mechanisms behind these adaptations using a wide range of approaches, from molecular biology to genomics and behavioral analysis.
